## 🗓️ Daily Study Routine

### Monday-Friday (2.5 hours/day)

**Morning (1 hour): New Questions**
- Complete one paper section (20 questions)
- Strict timing: 75 minutes
- Mark immediately after

**Afternoon (1 hour): Deep Review**
- Go through solutions carefully
- Understand WHY each wrong answer was wrong
- Find fastest solution method
- Create 3-5 Anki cards

**Evening (30 min): Reinforcement**
- Review all due Anki cards
- Quick mental math practice
- Re-attempt yesterday's errors

### Saturday (3 hours)

**Full Exam Simulation**
- Both papers (2h 30m)
- Exam conditions: quiet, no breaks, timed
- Mark and review (30 min)

### Sunday (1-2 hours)

**Review & Planning**
- Update progress tracker
- Review week's Anki cards
- Plan next week
- Rest the brain!

**Total:** 14-15 hours/week

## 🎓 Anki Strategy

### Card Creation

**From each paper, create cards for:**
1. **Concepts misunderstood**
   - Front: "What does [concept] mean?"
   - Back: Clear explanation + example
   
2. **Formula/theorem cards**
   - Front: "Formula for [situation]?"
   - Back: Formula + when to use it
   
3. **Common mistake cards**
   - Front: "What's the trap in [question type]?"
   - Back: What you did wrong + correct approach
   
4. **Pattern recognition cards**
   - Front: Question type description
   - Back: Fastest solution method

### Review Schedule

- **New cards per day:** 5-10 (no more!)
- **Daily reviews:** ALL due cards (usually 20-40)
- **Review time:** Every evening (15-30 min)
- **Never skip:** Even on rest days, review Anki

## ⚠️ Critical Don'ts

❌ **Don't skip the review process**
- Marking and moving on = wasted practice
- Deep review = actual learning

❌ **Don't do papers in wrong order**
- Save recent TMUA papers for last
- Don't "waste" them early

❌ **Don't skip Anki**
- Forgetting = relearning = wasted time
- Anki prevents this!

❌ **Don't burn out**
- 2-3 hours/day is enough
- Rest days matter
- Consistency > intensity

❌ **Don't ignore the digital interface**
- TMUA is computer-based!
- Practice on Pearson VUE essential
